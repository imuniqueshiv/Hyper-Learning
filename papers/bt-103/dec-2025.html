BT-103 (GS) – English for Communication

B.Tech. I/II Semester
Examination, December 2025
Grading System (GS)
Max Marks: 70 | Time: 3 Hours

Note:
(i) Attempt any five questions.
(ii) All questions carry equal marks.

Previous Year Questions (December 2025)

Q.1

a) Explain subject-verb agreement with suitable examples. (Unit 1)

b) Explain the correct use of articles with suitable examples. (Unit 1)
Q.2

a) Explain reported speech with suitable examples. (Unit 1)

b) Differentiate between active and passive voice with illustrations. (Unit 1)
Q.3

a) Explain prefixes and suffixes used in English vocabulary with five examples for each. (Unit 2)

b) Write short notes on synonyms and antonyms and write five examples for each. (Unit 2)
Q.4

a) What is reading comprehension? (Unit 2)

b) Explain methods to improve reading comprehension skills. (Unit 2)
Q.5

a) Define communication and discuss its importance for technical students. (Unit 3)

b) Explain the process of communication with a neat diagram. (Unit 3)
Q.6

a) Explain non-verbal communication with examples. (Unit 3)

b) Discuss barriers to communication and methods to overcome them. (Unit 3)
Q.7

a) Explain the steps involved in planning, drafting and editing. (Unit 4)

b) Write a short note on précis writing or technical description. (Unit 4)
Q.8

a) Explain the structure of a formal report. (Unit 4)

b) Write short notes on resume writing and business letters. (Unit 5)
