BT-103 (GS) – English for Communication

B.Tech. I & II Semester
Examination, June 2024
Grading System (GS)
Max Marks: 70 | Time: 3 Hours

Note:
i) Attempt any five questions.
ii) All questions carry equal marks.

Previous Year Questions

Q.1.

a) Determine whether each of the following statements is imperative, declarative, interrogative or exclamatory:
i) Let's walk together.
ii) What can one person do?
iii) We have made progress.
iv) How utterly we have failed our children!
v) What a big challenge it is!
vi) Kill not your children because of poverty.
vii) Do you know your multiplication tables?

b) Fill in the blanks as instructed:
i) The box of chocolates ______ (was/were) left on the kitchen counter.
ii) We went to ______ Concert last night. (a, an, the)
iii) Mathematics ______ (is/are) not my favorite subject in school.
iv) The Team ______ (is/are) practicing hard for the upcoming match.
v) Nobody ______ (know, knows) the trouble I've seen.
vi) The committee ______ (debates, debate) these questions careful.
vii) The scientist looked ______ the microscope. (through/in)

Q.2.

a) What is the synonyms and antonyms? Give five examples.

b) What is the difference between a prefix and a suffix? Give examples.
Q.3.

a) What is the importance of communication to students? Explain.

b) What obstacles exist in the way of communication? Explain strategies for overcoming them.
Q.4.

a) Write a brief essay about precis and precise writing.

b) What is the main purpose of a progress report? Explain.
Q.5.

a) Write a detailed essay about the significance of business letters.

b) What is the difference between limited and open tender? Describe.
Q.6.

a) Which characteristics distinguish a news report? What components and formats make up a news report?

b) Provide five reasons why you believe effective communication is essential.
Q.7.

a) What motivates the creation of technical definitions? What comprises a technical definition?

b) You wish to do a Spanish learning course from the institute 'Learn a Language'. Write a letter to enquire about course details, fees, duration etc. Sign yourself as Kritika/Kartik.
Q.8.

Provide the response (any two):

  • a) Trouble report
  • b) Guidelines for writing a resume
  • c) Drafting and Editing
  • d) Structure of a formal report
