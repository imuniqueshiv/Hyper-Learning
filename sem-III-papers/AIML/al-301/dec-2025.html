AI301/AL301(GS) – Technical Communication

B.Tech. III Semester
Examination, December 2025
Grading System (GS)
Max Marks: 70 | Time: 3 Hours

Note:
i) Attempt any five questions.
ii) All questions carry equal marks.

Previous Year Questions (December 2025)

Q.1

a) Explain the importance of communication in a globalized professional environment. (Unit 1)

b) Discuss the role of unity, brevity and clarity in technical communication. (Unit 1)
Q.2

a) Differentiate between verbal and non-verbal communication with suitable examples. (Unit 2)

b) Explain cultural barriers in global communication and how to overcome them. (Unit 2)
Q.3

a) Discuss the principles and traits of effective technical writing. (Unit 3)

b) Explain the format and structure of a technical report. (Unit 3)
Q.4

a) What is audience awareness? Explain its role in effective public speaking. (Unit 3)

b) Describe techniques to combat nervousness during presentations. (Unit 3)
Q.5

a) Explain the process of preparing for a job interview. (Unit 4)

b) Discuss the importance of non-verbal cues during interviews. (Unit 4)
Q.6

a) Define group discussion. Explain its objectives and evaluation criteria. (Unit 4)

b) Discuss argumentation and content skills required for effective group discussion. (Unit 4)
Q.7

a) Explain common grammatical errors in professional communication. (Unit 5)

b) Discuss the importance of vocabulary and pronunciation etiquette. (Unit 5)
Q.8

a) Describe the role of tone and flow in effective speaking. (Unit 5)

b) Explain how speech contributes to professional personality development. (Unit 5)
