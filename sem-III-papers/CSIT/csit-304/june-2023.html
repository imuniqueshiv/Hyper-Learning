Previous Year Questions (June 2023)

Q.1

a) Convert the following: (Unit 1)

i) $(B2F8)_{16} = (?)_{8}$

ii) $(113.2)_{8} = (?)_{2}$

iii) $(01000111)_{2}$ ------ $(.........)_{Gray}$

iv) $(249)_{10}$ ----------- $(.........)_{BCD}$

v) $(A2B.1A)_{16} = (?)_{10}$

b) Convert the following expression into sum of product and product of sums. (Unit 1)

$X' + X (X + Y') (Y + Z')$

a) What do you mean by logic gates? Describe the various types of gates. (Unit 1)

Q.3

a) Draw and explain the BCD adder circuit. (Unit 2)

i) 16:1 MUX

Q.4

b) Explain the principle working of sample and hold circuit. (Unit 4)
Q.6

a) With the help of Block diagram explain the working of BPSK modulation scheme. (Unit 5)

i) Schmitt trigger (Unit 4)

iii) Look ahead carry generator (Unit 2)
