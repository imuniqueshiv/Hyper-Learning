AL/CD/CS/CT/CO-403 (GS) – Software Engineering

B.Tech. IV Semester
Examination, November 2023
Grading System (GS)
Max Marks: 70 | Time: 3 Hours

Note:
i) Attempt any five questions.
ii) All questions carry equal marks.

Previous Year Questions (November 2023)

Q.1

a) What is spiral model? Explain with neat diagram. (Unit 1)

b) What is SDLC? Illustrate the phases of SDLC in software engineering. (Unit 1)
Q.2

a) What is the difference between function oriented modeling and object oriented modeling? (Unit 2)

b) Discuss about system and software requirement specifications. (Unit 2)
Q.3

a) What is a design metrics? Explain the importance of design metrics. (Unit 3)

b) Discuss about the function oriented design strategies in detail. (Unit 3)
Q.4

a) What is software testing and system testing? Explain in detail. (Unit 4)

b) Discuss about integration testing with suitable example. (Unit 4)
Q.5

a) What is risk and how to identify the risk in software engineering? Explain. (Unit 5)

b) Write the difference between reverse engineering and re-engineering. (Unit 5)
Q.6

a) What are the steps of acceptance testing? Explain in detail. (Unit 4)

b) Explain the principles of risk assessment and mitigation. (Unit 5)
Q.7

a) Write about project scheduling and tracking. (Unit 5)

b) Describe RUP and agile processes in detail. (Unit 1)
Q.8

Write a short note on any two:

a) Structured methods (Unit 3)

b) White-Box Testing (Unit 4)

c) Feasibility Analysis (Unit 5)

d) Test Case Design (Unit 4)
