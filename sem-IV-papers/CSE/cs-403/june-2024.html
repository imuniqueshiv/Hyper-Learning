AL/CD/CS/CT/CO/SD-403 (GS) – Software Engineering

B.Tech. IV Semester
Examination, June 2024
Grading System (GS)
Max Marks: 70 | Time: 3 Hours

Note:
i) Attempt any five questions.
ii) All questions carry equal marks.

Previous Year Questions (June 2024)

Q.1

a) Elucidate the key features of the software process models with suitable examples? (Unit 1)

b) Write about CMM (Capability Maturity Model) and how it is used for Software Quality? (Unit 1)
Q.2

a) Draw and explain the spiral model with its advantages and disadvantages? (Unit 1)

b) Discuss the components of a software requirement specification document? (Unit 2)
Q.3

a) Differentiate between functional and Non Functional requirements with suitable examples? (Unit 2)

b) What are the activities of requirement elicitation and Analysis? Explain. (Unit 2)
Q.4

a) Discuss about pattern based software design in detail? (Unit 3)

b) Briefly explain about Function-Oriented design? (Unit 3)
Q.5

a) List and explain the golden rules of User-Interface design? (Unit 3)

b) Briefly discuss about Integration testing Strategies? (Unit 4)
Q.6

a) What is Black- Box testing? What is boundary value Analysis? Explain the technique Specifying rules and its usage with the help of an example. (Unit 4)

b) What is White-Box testing? How white box testing is carried out? Demonstrate With example. (Unit 4)
Q.7

a) Explain briefly about Software Configuration Management (SCM)? (Unit 5)

b) What do you understand by Feasibility Analysis? Explain. (Unit 5)
Q.8

Write short notes on any two:

a) Schedule and Cost Estimation (Unit 5)

b) RUP (Unit 1)

c) Traceability (Unit 2)

d) Design Principles (Unit 3)
