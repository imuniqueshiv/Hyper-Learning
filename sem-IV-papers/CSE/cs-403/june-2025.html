AL/CD/CS/CT/CO/SD-403 (GS) – Software Engineering

B.Tech./B.Tech. (Working Professional) IV Semester
Examination, June 2025
Grading System (GS) / Working Professional
Max Marks: 70 | Time: 3 Hours

Note:
i) Attempt any five questions.
ii) All questions carry equal marks.

Previous Year Questions (June 2025)

Q.1

a) What are the five major activities of an operating system in regards to memory management? (Unit 1)

b) List the functions of OS. How operating system perform management task in a system? (Unit 1)
Q.2

a) What is the Component Assembly Model? How does it differ from the Incremental Model? (Unit 1)

b) Explain the purpose of process customization and improvement with reference to CMM. (Unit 1)
Q.3

a) Describe functional and non-functional requirements with examples. (Unit 2)

b) What are the major requirement elicitation techniques? Discuss any two in detail. (Unit 2)
Q.4

a) Explain use case modeling with the help of an example. (Unit 2)

b) What are the key contents of a Software Requirement Specification (SRS) document? (Unit 2)
Q.5

a) Describe any four fundamental software design principles. (Unit 3)

b) Explain the SA/SD approach to function-oriented design. (Unit 3)
Q.6

a) What are architectural views? Briefly describe different architectural styles. (Unit 3)

b) What are the key design metrics used to evaluate software design quality? (Unit 3)
Q.7

a) Differentiate between static and dynamic code analysis. (Unit 4)

b) Describe the black-box testing technique with an example. (Unit 4)
Q.8

a) What is Software Configuration Management (SCM)? Describe its major activities. (Unit 5)

b) Explain software project feasibility analysis and its types. (Unit 5)
