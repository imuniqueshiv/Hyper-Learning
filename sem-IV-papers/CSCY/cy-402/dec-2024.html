CY-402 (GS) – Fundamental of Cyber Security

B.Tech. IV Semester
Examination, December 2024
Grading System (GS)
Max Marks: 70 | Time: 3 Hours

Note:
i) Answer any five questions.
ii) All questions carry equal marks.

Previous Year Questions (December 2024)

Q.1

a) Define Cyber Crime. Classify the different types of Cyber Crimes. (Unit 1)

b) Write short notes on the Internet Time Theft. (Unit 1)
Q.2

a) What are the distinguishing characteristics between email spoofing and spamming, and how do these practices impact cyber security measures and user trust in online communication? (Unit 1)

b) Differentiate Hackers and extremist groups. (Unit 2)
Q.3

a) What are the different types of computer network intrusion techniques? Explain how we prevent them with suitable examples? (Unit 2)

b) How does web jacking differ from traditional hacking methods, and what are the specific techniques and vulnerabilities exploited in web jacking attacks? (Unit 2)
Q.4

a) What are the different strategies followed to tackle the Cyber Crimes? Explain them. (Unit 3)

b) How has the proliferation of teenage web vandals impacted online communities and digital spaces and what measures can be implemented to mitigate their influence and protect internet integrity? (Unit 3)
Q.5

a) How the nature of criminality effects the Cyber Crime? Provide necessary examples. (Unit 3)

b) Compare and contrast The Indian Evidence Act of 1872 v/s Information Technology Act, 2000. (Unit 4)
Q.6

a) Explain in brief about the role of proving electronic messages in the Cyber Crime. (Unit 4)

b) Write short notes on the electronic agreements as the proofs of the Cyber Crime. (Unit 4)
Q.7

a) How has the evolution of computer viruses influenced the cybercrime and what are the most significant challenges in detecting and mitigating the viruses? (Unit 5)

b) Write short notes on the DDoS Attacks. (Unit 5)
Q.8

Write short notes on any two of the following:

i) Salami Technique (Unit 1)

ii) Software Piracy (Unit 2)

iii) Cyber Fraud (Unit 3)

iv) Trojan Horses (Unit 5)
