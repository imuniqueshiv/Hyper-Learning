CY-402 (GS) – Fundamental of Cyber Security

B.Tech. IV Semester
Examination, November 2023
Grading System (GS)
Max Marks: 70 | Time: 3 Hours

Note:
i) Answer any five questions.
ii) All questions carry equal marks.

Previous Year Questions (November 2023)

Q.1

a) Explain cyber crime. Mention some important cyber crime and explain each of them. (Unit 1)

b) Explain E-mail spoofing, spamming. (Unit 1)
Q.2

a) Explain web jacking along with the prevention measure of online fraud and software piracy. (Unit 2)

b) What is theft in cyber terrorism also explain web server hacking? (Unit 2)
Q.3

a) Explain criminal justice with some of the main provisions of cyber crime in IT Act 2000. (Unit 3)

b) Explain Dos and DDos attacks in brief. What are the techniques behind such attacks? (Unit 5)
Q.4

a) Explain Indian Evidence Act 1872. What are the provisions mention in it. (Unit 4)

b) What do you understand by attack on web server? Explain the steps taken to avoid web server hacking. (Unit 2)
Q.5

a) List down some of the strategies to tackle cyber crime. (Unit 3)

b) Explain the term 'Hacking'. What are the various classification of hacker? Mention them. (Unit 3)
Q.6

a) What do you mean by 'Salami attack'? What are the adverse effect on economic structure of society. Explain with example. (Unit 1)

b) Explain defamation in the context of cyber crime. Mention the provision of cyber defamation. (Unit 3)
Q.7

a) What do you understand by the term VIRUS and WORMS along with their types in details. (Unit 5)

b) Explain "Software Piracy" write down various activities that constitutes software piracy. (Unit 2)
Q.8

a) How to find record as evidence to proof and management of electronic record for relevancy. (Unit 4)

b) Explain Session Hijacking with the method of initiating session hijacking. (Unit 2)
